# KK Designs — Designing Spaces That Tell Your Story

> *"At KK Designs, we believe that design is far more than an arrangement of furniture or a response to functional needs—it is an emotional journey. Every room, pillar, and material choice holds the power to capture memories and preserve personal heritage."*

**KK Designs** is a bespoke interior architecture and design studio founded by **Keerthika**, dedicated to crafting bespoke residential, commercial, and hospitality spaces that resonate with deep personal narratives. We bridge human emotion with functional elegance, transforming interiors into timeless environments where stories unfold and memories are passed down through generations.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Architectural Components

This web application replicates and elevates the signature digital components of luxury ateliers like [Essajees Atelier](https://www.essajeesatelier.com/), transformed into a bespoke Haute-Couture Editorial experience:

- **Immersive Hero Experience:** Features the hallmark tagline *"Designing Spaces That Tell Your Story"*, cinematic twilight architectural photography, animated spatial metrics, and an ambient rotating Sacred Geometry Sri Yantra mandala watermark.
- **The Philosophy & Founder Spotlight:** Features Keerthika's design manifesto, editorial atelier portrait, studio philosophy, and key spatial achievements.
- **Interactive "Before & After" Transformation Slider:** A split-screen comparison canvas allowing clients to interactively drag between raw architectural concrete shells (*Before*) and finished bespoke living environments (*After*) with multi-space selector presets.
- **Curated Portfolio & Spatial Story Lightbox:** Filterable by *Residential Havens, Hospitality & Lounges, Heritage Restorations,* and *Commercial Ateliers*, with rich modal walkthroughs revealing materials, volume planning, and emotional heritage preservation.
- **The 4-Pillar Design Process:** A guided roadmap through *Narrative Discovery, Spatial Choreography, Artisanal Materiality,* and *Generational Handover*.
- **Bespoke Services Grid:** End-to-end spatial solutions with custom architectural millwork, lighting automation, and heritage advisory.
- **Editorial Press & Recognition:** Featured mentions and critical praise from *Architectural Digest India, Elle Décor, Vogue Living,* and *GoodHomes*.
- **"Design Your Story" Interactive Project Estimator:** Dynamic project scale slider and aesthetic narrative mood selectors calculating turnkey timelines and craftsmanship tiers.
- **Direct Concierge & Private Consultation Booking:** Seamless scheduling with instant validation feedback, direct email (`keerthika.udt@gmail.com`), phone/WhatsApp (`+91 8171304776`), and the sacred geometry background motif.
- **Dual Luxury Themes:** Instant toggle between **Obsidian Nighttime Lounge** (Dark Mode) and **Warm Cashmere Alabaster** (Light Mode) with persistent local storage.
- **Comprehensive Cross-Device & Screen Compatibility:** Engineered with CSS `clamp()`, safe-area insets (`env(safe-area-inset-*)`), touch-action drag optimizations, and mobile drawer menu for flawless presentation from 320px foldables to 4K displays.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Markup & Structure:** Semantic HTML5 with WCAG accessibility standards and Open Graph SEO meta tags.
- **Styling:** Vanilla CSS3 with custom design tokens, fluid clamp typography, backdrop-filters, and multi-tier media queries.
- **Logic & Interactions:** Modern Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+) with touch event listeners, Intersection Observer for metric counters, and localStorage theme persistence.
- **Brand Assets:** Vector SVGs of the stylized KK double-K serif monogram and Sacred Geometry Sri Yantra mandala motif.
